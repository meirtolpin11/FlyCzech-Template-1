Installation - FTP Upload

To begin, please follow the steps below:

  • Launch your FTP manager software.
  • Connect to your hosting account using the provided FTP credentials (host, username, password).
  • Once connected, navigate to the desired directory where you want to upload the files. In most cases, this directory is called "public_html" or "www".
  • Locate the files you want to upload from the "maha" folder on your local computer.
  • Select the files and drag them into the FTP manager's file browser or right-click on the files and choose the "Upload" option.
  • Once the upload is complete, the files from the "maha" folder will be successfully uploaded to the specified directory on your hosting account.
