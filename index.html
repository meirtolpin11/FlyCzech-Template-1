Our Team

Our team, united by a passion for aviation, founded FlyCzech in 2018 to pass on our enthusiasm and contribute to the education of future aviation professionals. We aim to uphold the proud tradition of Czech aviation, improve safety, and provide high-quality training through close cooperation with airlines and aviation organizations.

Josef Pavlíček

Co-founder & Chief Pilot

Originally an aircraft mechanic and pilot, in addition to working for Czech Airlines and Travel Service / Smartwings, he also worked in so-called third world countries. He participated in the implementation of ICAO standards in the African Congo. He then got a taste of flying in military conflicts and returned to the Czech Republic after working in Dubai to pass on his experience.

Robert Scribuck

Co-founder & CEO

He has been flying since 2011, more often than seen he is heard - as an AFIS dispatcher or as an air traffic controller. Throughout his life, he gradually gained experience as an aviation freelancer, ranging from aircraft avionics maintenance and pilot training to the development and maintenance of flight simulators around the world. Together with Josef, Robert is responsible for the complete management of the company, you can meet him at most Fly Czech activities.
