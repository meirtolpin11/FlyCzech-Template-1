Who We are?

At FlyCzech, we care, you fly. We offer a personalized approach to flight training, focusing on quality, not quantity. Our modern, well-maintained aircraft and comprehensive services are designed to meet the needs of aspiring pilots and aviation enthusiasts at affordable prices. Located at Prague Letňany Airport, we provide a welcoming and supportive environment for every student, tailoring each lesson to your unique goals. Unlike larger flight schools, we take the time to nurture each individual, ensuring you get the attention and training you deserve. In a competitive aviation market, we stand out by offering exceptional customer service and a genuine passion for flying.

How to Obtain Your EASA PPL at Flyczech

At Flyczech, we offer a comprehensive and structured approach to earning your EASA Private Pilot License (PPL). Follow these six essential steps, from theory to flight, to become a certified pilot!

  • 1
    Ground School

    Start your journey with ground school, where you’ll learn essential theoretical knowledge about aviation, flight principles, navigation, meteorology, air law, and more.

  • 2
    Flight Training

    Begin your practical flight training with our experienced instructors. You'll build hands-on flying skills, learning everything from take-offs to landings and maneuvers in various conditions.

  • 3
    Solo Flights

    After mastering the basics, you'll take your first solo flight—a key milestone that demonstrates your ability to fly independently and safely.

  • 4
    Exams & English Proficiency

    Pass the required theoretical exams covering topics such as air law, navigation, and meteorology. Additionally, demonstrate your English proficiency, a requirement for international flight operations.

  • 5
    Radio Communication Exam

    Complete the radio communication exam to ensure you can effectively communicate with air traffic control during flights.

  • 6
    Flight Test & Certification

    Once all exams are complete, undergo a flight test with a qualified examiner. After passing, you’ll receive your EASA Private Pilot License (PPL) and be ready to take to the skies!

Our Team

Our team, united by a passion for aviation, founded FlyCzech in 2018 to pass on our enthusiasm and contribute to the education of future aviation professionals. We aim to uphold the proud tradition of Czech aviation, improve safety, and provide high-quality training through close cooperation with airlines and aviation organizations.

Robert Scribuck

Co-founder & CEO

He has been flying since 2011, more often than seen he is heard - as an AFIS dispatcher or as an air traffic controller. Throughout his life, he gradually gained experience as an aviation freelancer, ranging from aircraft avionics maintenance and pilot training to the development and maintenance of flight simulators around the world. Together with Josef, Robert is responsible for the complete management of the company, you can meet him at most Fly Czech activities.

Josef Pavlíček

Co-founder & Chief Pilot

Originally an aircraft mechanic and pilot, in addition to working for Czech Airlines and Travel Service / Smartwings, he also worked in so-called third world countries. He participated in the implementation of ICAO standards in the African Congo. He then got a taste of flying in military conflicts and returned to the Czech Republic after working in Dubai to pass on his experience.
